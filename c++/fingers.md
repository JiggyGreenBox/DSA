To reach high speeds for DSA and development, you need to assign every key to a specific finger. This ensures you aren't "crossing over" and allows your brain to map the keyboard like a grid.

The most important thing to remember is the **Home Row** (the middle row), where your fingers always return after hitting a key.

---

### The Left Hand (Home: A S D F)

| Finger | Home Key | Responsible For... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pinky** | **A** | `Q`, `A`, `Z`, `1`, `Tab`, `Caps Lock`, `Shift`, `Ctrl` |
| **Ring** | **S** | `W`, `S`, `X`, `2` |
| **Middle** | **D** | `E`, `D`, `C`, `3` |
| **Index** | **F** | `R`, `T` (Reach right), `F`, `G` (Reach right), `V`, `B`, `4`, `5` |

### The Right Hand (Home: J K L ;)

| Finger | Home Key | Responsible For... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Index** | **J** | `Y`, `U` (Reach left), `H`, `J` (Reach left), `N`, `M`, `6`, `7` |
| **Middle** | **K** | `I`, `K`, `,` (Comma / `<`), `8` |
| **Ring** | **L** | `O`, `L`, `.` (Period / `>`), `9` |
| **Pinky** | **;** | `P`, `;`, `/` (Forward slash / `?`), `0`, `-`, `=`, `[`, `]`, `'`, `Enter`, `Shift` |

### The Thumbs
* **Both Thumbs:** Responsible **only** for the **Spacebar**. Most people naturally favor one thumb (usually the right), which is perfectly fine.



---

### Critical Keys for DSA
Since you are likely coding in C++, Java, or Python, your **Right Pinky** is doing 40% of the heavy lifting. You should practice these specific movements until they are instant:

1.  **The "Brace Reach":** Moving your right pinky from `;` up to `[` and `]` (for arrays) or `Shift + [` for `{` (for code blocks).
2.  **The "Semicolon Snap":** In languages like C++ or Java, your right pinky should naturally rest right on the `;` key.
3.  **The "Shift Sync":** Use your **Left Pinky** to hold `Shift` when you need a symbol on the right side of the keyboard (like `(` or `{`), and use your **Right Pinky** for `Shift` when you need symbols on the left (like `!`, `@`, `#`).

### Pro Tip for Accuracy
If you find yourself hitting the wrong keys, check your **Index Fingers**. If they aren't anchored on the **F** and **J** "nubs," your whole hand is likely shifted one column to the left or right, which causes every single character to be a typo.
