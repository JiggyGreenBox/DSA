### One more suggestion

For each problem, don't just keep the code. Create a one-page "Greedy Notebook" with four lines:

* **Observation:** What key insight makes greediness work?
* **Greedy choice:** What decision do we make at each step?
* **Why is it safe?** (one-sentence exchange argument or invariant)
* **Complexity:** Time and space.

I think that notebook will be far more valuable during your final interview revision than revisiting full solutions repeatedly. It helps build the pattern recognition that's essential for greedy problems.
